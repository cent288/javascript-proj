About LSPU Supreme Student Council

The Supreme Student Council (SSC) of Laguna State Polytechnic University (LSPU) is the highest governing student body that represents the voice of the students.

We are committed to promoting leadership, service, and academic excellence. Our mission is to build a bridge between the administration and the student body, ensuring that every student’s welfare, rights, and needs are heard and addressed.

Through various programs, activities, and initiatives, the LSPU SSC fosters student engagement, strengthens unity, and upholds the values of integrity, inclusivity, and responsibility.

Our Leaders

President

Leads the SSC and represents the student body.

Vice President

Supports the president and manages projects.

Secretary

Maintains records and communication.

Treasurer

Manages funds and ensures transparency.

Auditor

Oversees accountability and financial integrity.
